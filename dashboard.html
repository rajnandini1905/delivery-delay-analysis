Key insights

  • Weather is the biggest controllable lever (distance is fixed): SLA breach rises from 17.4% (clear) to 66.6% in heavy rain.
  • New riders (<3 months) breach 36.0% of orders versus 23.0% for 12+ month riders.
  • Worst peak-hour hotspot: Electronic City | Heavy Rain at 100.0% breach.
  • Top delay driver by standardised effect: Distance, then Prep time.
  • Cancellations cost an estimated ₹97,518 (1.6% of GMV).

Recommendations

  • Add surge rider incentives and dynamic ETA buffers for Rain/Heavy Rain in outer zones.
  • Pair new riders with short-distance, low-congestion orders for their first 3 months.
  • Renegotiate prep-time SLAs with the slowest restaurants; pause promos on them at peak.
  • Pre-position riders in Whitefield, Electronic City and Hebbal before 12:00 and 19:00.
